App Features

  1. Periodic Vibration Reminder - The app vibrates every 30 minutes to remind users to keep their phone away from their body. - The vibration will *continue* until the user manually turns it
  2. Overheat Detection & Alerts - The app monitors battery temperature and if it exceeds 37°C, it triggers an alert. - Sends a notification warning the user about overheating.
  3. Ultra Power-Saving Mode - When the phone’s temperature exceeds 37°C, it opens the power-saving settings to reduce overheating.
  4. Simple UI - A basic interface with buttons to start and stop the safety service.
